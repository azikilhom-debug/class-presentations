Self-study · 7 hours per topic

Same three tasks for each of the ten graded topics, plus 20 hours of exam preparation. 90 hours in total.

AStudy the theory for the topic
BComplete the practical problem set
CAdvance your own data project

The data project runs all semester: one question, one dataset, one written conclusion. Submitted as a presentation or a short visual report.
