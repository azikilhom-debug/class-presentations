What to say out loud

Most branches cluster between 25 and 45 mln. The distribution has a long right tail and one isolated bar far from the rest.

Bars touch, because revenue is continuous. Gaps between bars in a histogram mean empty ranges — never decoration.

THE ONE JUDGEMENT CALL How many bins?

The same data, three bin widths

Too wide · 2 bins

Shape destroyed. You learn only that most branches are small.

Right · 8 bins

Cluster, tail and gap all visible. ≈ √12 bins.

Too narrow · 20 bins

Noise. Every branch is its own bar and no pattern survives.

Start with √n bins, then adjust until the shape is stable — and state the bin width in the caption.

SHAPE The direction of the tail names the skew

Three shapes, three orderings

Symmetric
mean = median = mode

Heights, measurement error, exam scores in a well-set exam.

Right-skewed
mode < median < mean

Income, house prices, waiting times — and our café chain.

Left-skewed
mean < median < mode

Age at death, scores on an easy test, retirement ages.

MEASURING SHAPE Pearson's second coefficient

Putting a number on the skew

Sk = 3 (x̄ − median) s
Sk = 3 (35.75 − 30) ÷ 20.3
Sk = 17.25 ÷ 20.3
Sk = +0.85
How to read it
Sk ≈ 0symmetric
0.5 to 1moderate right skew
> 1strong right skew
negativetail runs left

The sign always follows the tail. Positive means the long tail points right — towards the large values.

Reading it in three sentences

Centre. The median branch takes 30 mln a month.

Spread. The middle half of branches sit in a narrow 12 mln band, from 26 to 38.

Shape. The right whisker is longer and one point sits alone at 96 — right-skewed, with one branch unlike the others.

A box plot is the fastest honest summary of one numeric variable — and the only chart that shows outliers by construction.
THE RULE

An outlier is a definition, not an opinion

Step 1 · IQR
38 − 26 = 12
Step 2 · 1.5 × IQR
1.5 × 12 = 18
Step 3 · fences
26 − 18 = 8
38 + 18 = 56
Step 4 · verdict
96 > 56
outlier

Lower fence Q1 − 1.5 × IQR, upper fence Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Everything outside is flagged for investigation — flagged, not deleted. Branch 12 is not an error; it is a mall location with different economics.

WHAT NOW? Four legitimate responses

You flagged an outlier. Then what?

Only one of these is never acceptable: dropping the point and not saying so.

01
Check it. Is it a typo — 96 instead of 9.6? A wrong unit? Half of all outliers die here.
02
Explain it. A mall branch is a different business model. That is a finding, not a nuisance.
03
Report both ways. "Mean 35.75 with all branches; 30.27 excluding the mall location." Let the reader judge.
04
Switch statistic. Use the median and the IQR, which the point cannot distort in the first place.
✕
Delete it quietly. This is the one that ends careers. Every exclusion goes in the write-up.

Rules for scatter plots

One dot per unit — twelve branches, twelve dots. Never join them with a line unless the x-axis is time.

Put the variable you think explains on the horizontal axis and the one being explained on the vertical.

More advertising goes with more revenue here — but nothing in this chart proves that advertising caused it. Week 13.
INTERPRETATION Say all four, every time

Four words describe any scatter plot

Direction

Do the dots rise or fall from left to right? Positive, negative, or none.

Ours: positive
Form

A straight band, a curve, or two separate clouds? Only straight bands justify a linear model.

Ours: roughly linear
Strength

How tightly do the dots hug the pattern? Week 6 gives this a number, r.

Ours: strong
Unusual points

Anything far from the crowd — it may be driving the whole apparent pattern.

CHART CLINIC Four ways to mislead without lying

Honest numbers, dishonest picture

Truncated axis

Starting the y-axis at 94 turns a 3% rise into a cliff. Bar charts must start at zero.

Area for one number

Doubling both width and height quadruples the ink for a doubled value.

Seven-slice pie

Nobody can rank these slices. A sorted bar chart answers it instantly.

Two axes, one frame

Sliding two scales until the lines cross manufactures any relationship you want.
