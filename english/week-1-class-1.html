CENTRE · 1 OF 3 The balance point

The arithmetic mean

x̄ = Σ xi n

Sample mean x̄ estimates the population mean μ. Same formula, different letter — the letter tells the reader whether you measured everyone or a sample.

Worked on our 12 cafés
x̄ = 429 ÷ 12
35.75

Million UZS per branch per month. Always restate the unit — a mean without units is not an answer.

CENTRE · 2 OF 3 The middle value

The median

Recipe
1 — Sort the values.
2 — Position = (n + 1) ÷ 2.
3 — Odd n: take that value.
4 — Even n: average the two either side.
Here n = 12, so position 6.5 — halfway between the 6th and 7th value.
Positions 6 and 7
(30 + 30) ÷ 2
30.0

Half the branches earn less than 30 mln, half earn more. The median ignores how much more — which is exactly why branch 12 cannot drag it.

A statistic that barely reacts to extreme values is called robust. The median is robust; the mean is not. Neither is wrong — but reporting the mean here would describe a chain none of the eleven other branches belongs to.

MEASURES OF SPREAD Three formulas to memorise

Range, variance, standard deviation

Range
max − min
96 − 18 = 78

Uses only two observations, so a single error destroys it. Quote it, never rely on it.

Variance
s2 = Σ (xi − x̄)2 n − 1
413.3

In squared units — "413 million UZS squared" means nothing to a manager. An intermediate step, not a report.

Standard deviation
s = √ s2
√413.3 = 20.3

Back in million UZS. Read it as: a typical branch sits about 20 mln away from the mean.

COMPARING SPREADS When the scales differ

Coefficient of variation

CV = (s ÷ x̄) × 100%. A unit-free measure — the only fair way to compare the variability of a café chain with that of a bank.

Café chain
x̄ = 35.75 mln
s = 20.3 mln
56.8%
Bank branches
x̄ = 1 240 mln
s = 186 mln
15.0%
Read it

The bank's standard deviation is nine times larger in absolute money, yet its branches are far more consistent relative to their own size.

Never compare two standard deviations across different units. Compare CVs.

Q3 = median of (30, 33, 36, 40, 44, 96) = (36 + 40) ÷ 2 = 38
The middle 50% of branches earn between 26 and 38 mln — a 12 mln band. Compare that with the range of 78, and you can already see that one observation is doing something unusual.
